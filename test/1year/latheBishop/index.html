04 / MACHINING PROCESS

CNC Machining Operations

The Lathe Bishop is manufactured through a sequence of turning operations. The operations below provide an overview of the machining process.

01. Stopper

Tool: T4 D1 — Sleeve (Stopper)
Workpiece positioning / stopper setting

02. Facing

Tool: T1 D1 — PCLNR 2020 K12
Constant surface speed: G96 S200 LIMS=1500
Spindle CW: M03
Facing passes at Z0.25 and Z0
Feed: F0.1

03. Contouring

Tool: T7 D1 — MVJNR 2020 K16
Constant surface speed: G96 S200 LIMS=1500
Spindle CW: M03
CYCLE95 stock-removal cycle
The contour is defined using AB:BC

04. Parting

Tool: T3 D1 — CFIR 2020 K06
Constant RPM: G97 M03 S1200
Spindle CW: M03
Variable R1 and IF/GOTO loop are used for the cutting sequence.
